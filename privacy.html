Privacy Policy — Little Genius Ultra

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Little Genius Ultra is an educational learning app designed for young children (Age 3+) and families. Protecting children's privacy is our top priority.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from children or adults. This includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers (AAID).

2. Local Device Storage Only

App progress (such as earned stars, daily adventure completion, and voice speed preference) is saved strictly on your own device using browser localStorage. No data is ever sent to external servers.

3. No Ads, No Tracking, No In-App Purchases

Little Genius Ultra contains zero third-party advertisements, zero analytics SDKs, and zero in-app purchases.

4. Parental Gate

Settings, progress reset, and external policy links are protected behind a math-based Parental Gate so only parents or guardians can access them.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
